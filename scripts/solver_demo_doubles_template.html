Doubles Solver Demo

In doubles each side picks an action for both active Pokémon at the same time, so a side's choices are every combination of its two slots. We play every pair of those joint actions under all chance outcomes, score the results, and solve the grid for each side's best mix. The grid is large, so this page also shows how much of it pruning can drop.

    How this fits together

    1. Joint actions. Each slot has a few moves (some with a choice of target) plus switches. A side's actions are the product of its two slots.
    2. One matrix cell = play the turn for that pair under every chance outcome (crit, accuracy, damage) and average the scores.
    3. Solve the matrix exactly for the mix neither side can improve on.
    4. Prune by keeping each side's top-k actions by average payoff plus every best reply, then solving the small game. If its value and support match the full game, nothing was lost.
    5. Two turns repeats all of this for every chance outcome: each outcome is a new position whose own matrix is solved, and the cell becomes the average of those values. Building a full matrix for every outcome would take days, so at every turn each Pokémon only considers its top two damaging moves (by expected damage), its best status move and its best switch. That filter is a heuristic, not a guarantee.
